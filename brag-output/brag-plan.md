# Grid Guardian launch film

## Angle

Treat Grid Guardian like a premium operations product trailer: a Night of Lights signal cuts through Glowtown, the real fictional dashboard wakes up, Lantern Row is selected, and a focused current briefing resolves. The workshop loop appears only as quick proof marks after the product payoff, never as a presentation or explainer.

## Visual identity

- Canvas: 1920x1080 landscape, 30 fps, 20.5 seconds.
- Palette: night navy `#080d1a`, lime `#BDDB00`, magenta `#DE0073`, blue-grey glass, warm amber warning.
- Typography: Inter-like system display face with JetBrains Mono for tiny labels.
- Product surface: faithful recreation of the visible Grid Guardian dashboard and repository fixtures.
- Fictional/public-safe data only. No incident IDs, internal notes, routes, credentials, metadata, or real operational data.
- Motion language: camera push-ins, light trails, living status dots, cursor selection, panel slide, restrained grain and depth.

## Storyboard

| Time | Beat | On-screen story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0-2.5 | Night signal | Dashboard is already the hero poster underneath a dark Glowtown atmosphere. Light trails wake up. `TONIGHT, THE CITY LIGHTS UP.` A small signal arrives. |
| 2.5-5.0 | Request lands | A request fragment enters the product context: `Lantern Row needs briefing.` The prompt hangs on `WHAT'S CURRENT?` while the dashboard focus shifts. |
| 5.0-9.0 | Dashboard wakes | The real fictional neighborhood rows pulse in: Lantern Row and Copper Park show `Attention needed`; Beacon Hill shows `Stable`. The cursor moves with purpose and selects Lantern Row. |
| 9.0-14.0 | Briefing payoff | The current briefing panel slides in and locks: `Lantern Row`, `Attention needed`, and `Temporary outage affects the east promenade.` The second public-safe summary glints as supporting context, but no metadata appears. |
| 14.0-18.0 | Proof marks | Kinetic micro-stamps cross the product surface: `SHAPED`, `TESTED`, `REVIEWED`, `GUARDED`, with a thin lime route through them. They read as engineering credibility, not slides. |
| 18.0-20.5 | Product lockup | Grid Guardian mark and wordmark land over the living dashboard. `DEV DAY / AI-ASSISTED` and `GLOWTOWN / NIGHT OF LIGHTS` remain tiny context labels. |

## Audio

- Music: local bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, low cinematic bed at 0.25 volume.
- SFX: local UI select/click, soft drops, and one restrained impact bell for the briefing lockup.
- Arc: low pulse under the hook, rising clicks through selection, warm impact at 11.2s, clean resolve under the logo.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the atmospheric halo and product glass breathe with the music bed. No waveform or equalizer graphics.
- Beat guidance: major reveal around 11.2s and logo around 19.0s are natural strong-cue approximations; readability wins over forced sync.

## Poster

The settled briefing dashboard at approximately 12.2s is the poster. It is extracted from the final render and overlaid onto frame 0 so idle players show the product, not a title card or transition.
